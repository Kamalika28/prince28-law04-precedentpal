# Dataset: PrecedentPal

A Supreme Court research assistant that finds precedents, separates majority and dissent, and checks whether a judgment was later overruled.

## Collection targets

- Start target: 1,000 constitutional judgments from 2015–2025.
- Full target: 20,000+ judgments from 2000–2025.
- Current collection: 55 processed PDFs. This is not yet a verified count of qualifying constitutional judgments.

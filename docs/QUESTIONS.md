# PrecedentPal evaluation questions

These questions form the initial retrieval and answer-quality test set. Answers must cite the source judgment and identify the opinion relied upon.

1. What constitutional issue did the Court decide in *Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala*?
2. What limits did the Court place on Parliament's amending power in *Kesavananda Bharati*?
3. How did *Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India* interpret the relationship between Articles 14, 19 and 21?
4. What principle concerning the basic structure was applied in *Minerva Mills v. Union of India*?
5. What safeguards against arbitrary arrest were laid down in *D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal*?
6. **Unanswerable from the indexed corpus:** What did the Supreme Court decide yesterday in a judgment that has not yet been ingested into PrecedentPal?
7. Which judges formed the Constitution Bench in *Kesavananda Bharati*, and how many judges sat?
8. What legal test did the Court apply in *Sharad Birdhichand Sarda v. State of Maharashtra* when assessing circumstantial evidence?
9. What was the central constitutional question in *S.R. Bommai v. Union of India*?
10. Which later judgments cite a selected precedent, and do they follow, distinguish, or overrule it?

## Evaluation rule

A response is not considered supported merely because it sounds plausible. It must provide a case name, citation or source link, and pinpoint reference where available. If the corpus cannot answer a question, the API or assistant must say so rather than invent an answer.

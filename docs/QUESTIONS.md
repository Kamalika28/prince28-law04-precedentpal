# Real user questions: PrecedentPal

Written the way my user types them. User: Third-year law student on a Chennai law college moot court team researching constitutional and criminal precedents.

These 10 questions become my first evaluation set in Week 3. Question 6 is not answerable from my documents: the product must say "I don't know".

| # | Question | Type | Where the answer is (document, page) | More than one page? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What did the Supreme Court hold in K.S. Puttaswamy (2017) about privacy? | simple | K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India (2017), privacy as a fundamental right | No |
| 2 | What is the basic structure doctrine? | simple | TKesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973), basic structure doctrine | No |
| 3 | Which judgments discuss bail as the rule and jail as the exception? | simple | State of Rajasthan v. Balchand (1977); Satender Kumar Antil v. CBI (2022) | No |
| 4 | Our moot problem is about a state law that bans an app citing public order. Which Supreme Court precedents on free speech and reasonable restrictions should we rely on, and which might the other side use? | multi-step (several pages) | Shreya Singhal v. Union of India (2015); Anuradha Bhasin v. Union of India (2020) | Yes |
| 5 | Compare the majority and dissenting opinions in a recent Constitution Bench judgment of your choice. | comparison | K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India (Aadhaar, 2018), majority opinion and Justice Chandrachud's dissent | Yes |
| 6 | How will the court decide our moot problem? | unanswerable: must reply "I don't know" | none: not in any document | - |
| 7 | Can the government restrict online speech if it claims that the content threatens public order? | simple | Shreya Singhal v. Union of India (2015), Article 19(1)(a) and Article 19(2) | Yes |
| 8 | What rules must the government follow before suspending internet services in a state? | simple | Anuradha Bhasin v. Union of India (2020), internet shutdown guidelines | Yes |
| 9 | Can private companies require customers to provide Aadhaar details for authentication? | simple | K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India (Aadhaar, 2018), Section 57 of the Aadhaar Act | Yes |
| 10 | Does filing a chargesheet automatically mean that the accused must be arrested? | simple | Satender Kumar Antil v. CBI (2022), arrest and bail guidelines | Yes |
